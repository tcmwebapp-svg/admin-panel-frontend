👥 Section 3: Resident Profile (Detailed)

The Resident Profile is the central hub for all information related to a specific resident. It is divided into several tabs for easy management:

1. About Me

This is the primary information page. It includes:

  • Personal Details: Full name, DOB, NHS number, and NI number.
  • Medical Profile: Primary diagnosis, diagnosis date, and a list of all allergies.
  • Contacts: Detailed info for Next of Kin (NOK), GP, and specialists.
  • Preferences: "Important To Me", "Please DO", and "Please DON'T" lists to personalize care.

Buttons: Edit to change info, Save to commit changes.

2. Care Plans

Manage structured care delivery plans.

  • Includes Communication, Mobility, Dietary, and Personal Care needs.
  • Tracks review dates to ensure plans are never outdated.

Buttons: + Add Care Plan, View History.

3. Medication (eMAR)

Full digital medication record.

  • Active Orders: See what medications are currently prescribed.
  • Admin Log: A history of every dose given, missed, or refused, including who gave it.

Buttons: GIVE, REFUSE.

4. Daily Logs & Handovers

Tracks the day-to-day life of the resident.

  • Daily Logs: Staff record mood, health indicators (HR, Bristol score), and notes.
  • Handovers: Summary notes passed between shifts to ensure continuity of care.

5. PBS, Risk & Goals

Clinical and behavioral management.

  • PBS Plans: Strategies for Positive Behaviour Support.
  • Risk Assessments: Identifying and mitigating risks like falls or injuries.
  • Goals: Setting and tracking outcomes for the resident's development.

👔 Section 9: Staff Profile (Detailed)

The Staff Profile manages all human resources data for each employee:

1. Personal & Employment

Core HR data including contact info, address, position, and contract type.

  • Compliance: Tracks DBS status, Right to Work, and Visa expiry.
  • Next of Kin: Emergency contact details for the staff member.

2. Scheduling & Rota

An interactive calendar showing the staff member's assigned shifts.

Action: Use the calendar to see upcoming duties or check past attendance.

3. Training & Performance

Ensuring staff are qualified and productive.

  • Training: Lists all completed and pending training modules with certificates.
  • Performance: Tracks Holiday allowance, Appraisals, and Supervisions.

Status Colors: Green (Current), Yellow (Expiring), Red (Expired).

4. Pay & Documents

Administrative and financial data.

  • Pay: Bank account details and tax codes for payroll.
  • Documents: A secure folder for contracts, ID, and disciplinary records.

📋 Section 6: Incident Reports

How to Report?

  1. Click the + New Incident button.

  4. Submit; Manager will receive an instant notification.

🔐 Section 13: User Management (Roles & Permissions)

This section allows the Administrator to control who can access the system and what they are allowed to see or change.

1. Administrator (Admin)

Role: Full System Controller.

  • Full Access: Can see and edit every module.
  • User Control: Only Admin can Add, Edit, or Delete other users.
  • Security: Can reset passwords and change system settings.
  • Deletion: Only Admin has the power to delete important records (Residents, Staff, Incident Reports).

2. Staff Member

Role: Daily Care Provider.

  • Operational Access: Can add Daily Logs, update Care Plans, and record Medication.
  • No Deletion: Cannot delete profiles or system-wide records.
  • Limited HR: Can see their own profile but cannot see other staff members' private data (like bank details).

3. External User (Auditor / Family)

Role: Guest Viewer.

  • Restricted Access: Can only see specific pages permitted by the Admin.
  • View-Only: Cannot add, edit, or delete anything.
  • Audit Ready: Perfect for CQC inspectors or family members who only need to check progress.

4. Client (Resident)

Role: Self-View (Optional).

  • Personal View: If given access, a resident can see their own care plan and progress.
  • Privacy: They cannot see any other resident's data or staff information.

Buttons: Add User, Manage Permissions, Remove Access.

🛠️ Detailed Module Guide (How it works)

This section explains the step-by-step workflow for the most important parts of the system.

1. Managing a Resident (Full Workflow)

Step 1: Add a new resident in Resident Management.

Step 2: Go to their profile and fill out About Me (Allergies are critical!).

Step 3: Create a Care Plan so staff know what to do.

Step 4: Perform Assessments (Nutrition, Skin, Risk) to identify any health dangers.

Step 5: Staff record Daily Logs every day to show they are providing care.

2. Giving Medication (eMAR)

Setup: First, add the medication to the resident's profile.

Execution: When it's time for medicine, find the resident in Medication Management, check the dose, and click GIVE.

Safety: If you cannot give the medicine, click REFUSE and type the reason (e.g., patient sleeping).

3. Reporting an Incident

Immediate Action: As soon as an accident happens, open Incident Reports.

Data Entry: Select the type of incident, write exactly what happened, and add any evidence (photos).

Review: The Manager checks the report and adds Follow-up actions to prevent it from happening again.

4. Audits & Compliance

Daily Routine: Use the Compliance module to record fire safety, water temp, and cleaning checks.

Evidence: Upload photos of signed sheets or meter readings as proof for inspectors.
