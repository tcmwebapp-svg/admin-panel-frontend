👥 Section 3: Resident Profile (Mukammal Tafseel)

Resident Profile kisi bhi mareez ki sari maloomat ka markaz hai. Ise mukhtalif tabs mein taqseem kiya gaya hai:

1. About Me (Mere Baare Mein)

Yeh bunyadi maloomat ka page hai. Isme shamil hai:

  • Zati Tafseel: Pura naam, DOB, NHS number, aur NI number.
  • Medical Profile: Bemari ki tashkhees (Diagnosis), diagnosis ki tareekh, aur allergies ki list.
  • Rabta (Contacts): Next of Kin (NOK), GP, aur specialists ki mukammal tafseel.
  • Pasand o Na-pasand: "Important To Me", "Please DO", aur "Please DON'T" ki lists taake care ko zati zaroryat ke mutabiq banaya ja sake.

Buttons: Edit maloomat badalne ke liye, Save tabdeeli ko mehfooz karne ke liye.

2. Care Plans (Care Planning)

Care delivery ke mansoobon ka intezam.

  • Isme Communication, Mobility, Dietary, aur Personal Care ki zaroryat shamil hain.
  • Review dates track ki jati hain taake plans purane na hon.

Buttons: + Add Care Plan, View History.

3. Medication (eMAR - Dawaiyan)

Dawaiyo ka digital record.

  • Active Orders: Dekhein ke kon si dawaiyan di ja rahi hain.
  • Admin Log: Har us dose ki history jo di gayi, reh gayi ya mana kar di gayi, staff ke naam ke sath.

Buttons: GIVE (Dawai di gayi), REFUSE (Mana kar diya).

4. Daily Logs aur Handovers

Resident ki rozana ki zindagi ka record.

  • Daily Logs: Staff resident ka mood, sehat ke indicators (HR, Bristol score), aur notes record karte hain.
  • Handovers: Aik shift se doosri shift ko di jane wali summary notes taake care musalsal rahe.

5. PBS, Risk aur Goals

Clinical aur behavioral management.

  • PBS Plans: Positive Behaviour Support ki strategies.
  • Risk Assessments: Girne ya chot lagne jaise khatraat ko pehchanna aur unhe kam karna.
  • Goals: Resident ki taraqqi ke liye maqasid set karna aur unhe track karna.

👔 Section 9: Staff Profile (Mukammal Tafseel)

Staff Profile har mulazim ke HR data ka intezam karti hai:

1. Personal & Employment (Zati aur Mulazmat)

Bunyadi HR data jisme rabta, pata, ohda (position), aur contract ki type shamil hai.

  • Compliance: DBS status, Right to Work, aur Visa expiry track karta hai.
  • Next of Kin: Emergency rabte ki tafseel.

2. Scheduling & Rota (Shifts ka Intezam)

Aik interactive calendar jo mulazim ki assigned shifts dikhata hai.

Action: Calendar ke zariye aane wali shifts dekhein ya purani attendance check karein.

3. Training & Performance (Training aur Karkardagi)

Staff ki qabiliyat aur karkardagi ko yaqeeni banana.

  • Training: Mukammal aur pending training modules ki list certificates ke sath.
  • Performance: Holiday allowance, Appraisals, aur Supervisions ka record.

Rangon ka Maatlab: Sabz (Mukammal), Peela (Khatam hone wali), Surkh (Khatam ho chuki).

4. Pay aur Documents

Admin aur maliyati (financial) data.

  • Pay: Bank account details aur payroll ke liye tax codes.
  • Documents: Contracts, ID, aur disciplinary records ke liye aik mehfooz folder.

🔐 Section 13: User Management (Roles & Permissions)

Yeh section Administrator ko ijazat deta hai ke wo faisla kare ke system kon istemal karega aur wo kya dekh ya badal sakta hai.

1. Administrator (Admin)

Role: Full System Controller.

  • Mukammal Ikhtiyar: Har module ko dekh aur badal sakta hai.
  • User Control: Sirf Admin hi naye users bana sakta hai ya purano ko nikal sakta hai.
  • Security: Passwords badalna aur system settings ko control karna.
  • Deletion: Sirf Admin ke paas zaroori record (Residents, Staff, Incidents) delete karne ki taqat hai.

2. Staff Member (Mulazim)

Role: Rozana ki Care dene wala.

  • Kaam ka Ikhtiyar: Daily Logs add karna, Care Plans update karna, aur Medication record karna.
  • Delete ki ijazat nahi: Wo profiles ya main records delete nahi kar sakta.
  • Limited HR: Wo apni profile dekh sakta hai lekin doosre staff ki private details (jise bank account) nahi dekh sakta.

3. External User (Auditor / Family)

Role: Guest Viewer (Sirf dekhne wala).

  • Mehdood Ikhtiyar: Sirf wahi pages dekh sakta hai jiski Admin ne ijazat di ho.
  • Sirf Dekhna: Wo kuch bhi add, edit ya delete nahi kar sakta.
  • Audit: Yeh inspectors (jise CQC) ya ghar walon ke liye behtareen hai jo sirf progress check karna chahte hain.

4. Client (Resident)

Role: Apni report dekhna.

  • Zati View: Agar ijazat ho, to resident apna care plan aur apni taraqqi dekh sakta hai.
  • Privacy: Wo kisi doosre resident ya staff ki maloomat nahi dekh sakta.

Buttons: Add User, Manage Permissions, Remove Access.

🛠️ Modules ka Mukammal Tariqa-e-Kar (Workflow)

Is section mein bataya gaya hai ke system ke ahem hisson mein kaam kaise hota hai.

1. Resident ka Record Kaise Manage Karein?

Step 1: Sab se pehle Resident Management mein naya resident add karein.

Step 2: Unki profile mein ja kar About Me bharain (Allergies likhna bohot zaroori hai!).

Step 3: Care Plan banayein taake staff ko pata ho ke kya karna hai.

Step 4: Assessments (Nutrition, Skin, Risk) karein taake khatraat ka pata chale.

Step 5: Staff rozana Daily Logs bharegi taake care ka saboot rahe.

2. Dawai Dene ka Tariqa (eMAR)

Setup: Pehle resident ki profile mein dawai add karein.

Amal: Dawai ke waqt Medication Management mein jayein aur GIVE par click karein.

Safety: Agar dawai nahi de sakte, to REFUSE par click karein aur wajah (reason) likhen.

3. Incident Report Kaise Karein?

Fauri Amal: Jaise hi koi haadsa ho, Incident Reports kholain.

Entry: Incident ki type select karein aur batayein ke kya hua, aur agar ho sake to photo upload karein.

Review: Manager report check karke Follow-up likhega taake aisa dobara na ho.

4. Audits aur Compliance

Rozana ka Kaam: Compliance module mein fire safety, pani ka temperature aur cleaning checks record karein.

Saboot: Signed sheets ya readings ki photo upload karein taake inspectors ko dikhaya ja sake.
